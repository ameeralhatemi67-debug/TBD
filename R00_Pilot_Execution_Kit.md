# R00 pilot execution kit (8 participants, directional)

**Date prepared:** 27 September 2026. **Based on:** [R00 §28–32](R00_User_Decision_Study.md#28-proposed-user-study) (study design, profiles, tasks, interview guide, observation template). This kit shortens those sections for an 8-person run.

**Status:** Ready to use. **No participant has been recruited, contacted, or observed.** Nothing below is a finding.

Eight sessions give **directional learning only**. They cannot validate a market, measure frequency in a population, or support percentages.

**Hypotheses under test** (R00):
- *First user:* a resident who makes near-term outing decisions for themselves or a small group.
- *First decision:* "Given where we start, when we can go, our time and budget, and what we feel like doing, which two or three credible options can we actually choose now?"

---

## 1. Recruitment screener (5 minutes, by message or call)

Ask all questions. Record answers without names in the sheet (use `P01`–`P08`).

1. Do you live in [Al Khobar / Dhahran / Dammam, or the area being studied]? *(Screen out if no.)*
2. In the last 4 weeks, how many times did you decide on an outing (not a routine errand) for yourself or with 1–5 others? *(0 / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6+)*
3. Who usually suggests the plan when you go out with others? *(Usually me / Sometimes me / Rarely me)*
4. The last time, who was with you? *(Alone / Partner / Friends / Family with children / Other)*
5. Which tools or people did you use to decide? *(Tick all that apply: Google Maps, Google Search, TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, WhatsApp or asking friends, webook, Visit Saudi or Enjoy, operator websites, other.)*
6. Preferred language for the session: Arabic / English / either.
7. Do you have an outing decision coming up in the next week that we could look at together, only if you're comfortable?
8. Any access, transport or budget constraints you'd like us to know about? *(Optional. Never required.)*

**Target mix for the eight** (adjusted from R00 §29; one person can fill two rows):

| Profile | Target |
|---|---:|
| Usually suggests outings for friends | 3 |
| Usually solo or with a partner | 2 |
| Plans family outings with children | 2 |
| Says they usually go to familiar places (a negative case) | 1 |
| Frequency spread | At least 2 answering 1–2 and at least 2 answering 6+ on Q2 |
| Tool spread | At least 2 whose first tool is social media, and at least 2 whose first tool is Maps or Search |
| Language | At least 2 Arabic-preferring |

Do **not** recruit only event enthusiasts or people who follow local-discovery accounts.

## 2. Recruitment message draft (for the owner to send; not sent)

> Hi [name], I'm doing a small research study on how people in [area] decide where to go out: an evening with friends, a family afternoon, a free couple of hours. It's a 30–40 minute conversation (in person or video) where you show me how you'd normally decide, using your own phone and apps.
>
> There's nothing to buy and no app to install. We aren't testing you. Recording is optional, and you can skip anything. [Incentive, if any: ___.]
>
> Interested? I'll send 8 short questions first.

## 3. Consent introduction (read at the start)

> Thank you for helping. We're learning how people decide on outings, not testing you or any product.
> - The session takes 30–40 minutes. You can stop or skip any question at any time.
> - **Recording is optional**, and so is **screen sharing**. If you'd rather not, I'll just take notes.
> - Please **don't show private messages, account details, payment information or exact home addresses.** If something private appears on screen, tell me and I'll stop and delete that part of my notes.
> - Nothing you do today makes a booking or purchase. Please don't book anything during the session.
> - I'll record your answers under a code (P01, etc.), not your name.
> - Short quotes may be used in internal notes, marked with your code, only if you agree now.
>
> Is that OK? Recording: yes / no. Screen sharing: yes / no. Quotes: yes / no.

Record the three answers on the observation sheet before starting.

## 4. Session script (30–40 minutes)

| Time | Part | What to do |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–3 | Consent | Section 3 |
| 3–8 | Recent outing | "Tell me about the last time you decided what to do outside home. What started it? Who suggested the option? What did you check?" (R00 §31) |
| 8–20 | **Task A: genuine upcoming decision** | "You mentioned [upcoming decision]. Please decide the way you normally would, using your own apps, and think out loud." If the participant has none, use card 2 or card 11 below. Stop at a choice, an explicit "none of these", or 12 minutes. |
| 20–30 | **Task B: assigned scenario** | Pick one card per participant and vary cards across participants. Personalise the area and time, but keep the constraint structure. |
| 30–36 | Follow-up | Section 5 questions |
| 36–40 | (Optional) Concept reaction | **Only after both tasks.** Show one plain printed example card: a short list of 2–3 options, each with "confirmed / not confirmed / check yourself" lines. Record the reaction **separately**. It is not task evidence. |

**Scenario cards for Task B** (from R00 §30):
- **Card 2, Thursday with friends:** "Two friends can meet Thursday evening. Find one thing you would genuinely suggest."
- **Card 3, 90-minute gap:** "You have 90 minutes before a dinner reservation near [place] at [time]. Decide whether to do anything beforehand."
- **Card 7, family fit:** "A family with children aged 6 and 9 needs a two-hour afternoon option, total budget under 300 SAR."
- **Card 11, bookable activity:** "Four people want an activity tonight without booking days in advance."
- **Card 12, no good option:** "Given these constraints [tight window], decide whether any option is worth going to."

**Rules:**
- Do not describe or show our concept before Task A and Task B are finished.
- Do not suggest tools or venues.
- Don't help unless the phone or app is technically stuck.
- Ask them not to book or pay.

## 5. Neutral prompts and follow-up questions

**During tasks** (R00 §31; use sparingly):
- "What are you looking for on this screen?"
- "What would you check next?"
- "What makes this one a possibility?"
- "What's still uncertain?"
- "Why did you open that?"
- "What would make you stop searching?"

**Avoid** leading questions such as "Wouldn't one app be easier?" or "Isn't that too far?"

**After tasks:**
1. "Which options did you consider, and why did you rule each out?"
2. "What made you comfortable with the final option?"
3. "What would you still check before leaving or paying?"
4. "How confident are you it would work, on your own scale? What would change that?"
5. "In the last four weeks, how often did a decision like this come up? Walk me through them."
6. "Which part took the most effort today, if any?"

**What would support, weaken or change the hypotheses.** These are directional, judged across 8 people. They are not significance tests.

| Hypothesis | Supported if… | Weakened if… | Would change the plan if… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First user:** residents deciding near-term outings for a small group | Several participants report multiple such decisions in 4 weeks, and the organiser role is recognisable | Most report 0–1 decisions in 4 weeks, or plans are fixed by habit | Visitors or families show harder, more frequent decisions. In that case, shift the first-user hypothesis. |
| **First decision:** choosing 2–3 credible options under time, budget and group constraints | Participants spend real effort on practical checks (hours, price for the group, sessions, travel) and reject options for those reasons | Most tasks end quickly and confidently in one tool (for example Maps alone), or rejections are about taste rather than facts | Rejections centre on novelty or quality, not feasibility. In that case, move emphasis toward promise B (something new locally). |
| **Remaining checks are the pain** (R00 H2) | Participants list several remaining checks and express uncertainty about them | Few remaining checks; confident choices | — |
| **Cross-type comparison** (R00 H3) | Participants compare venues, activities and events in one decision | They pick a category first and never compare across types | Scope narrows to one category. |
| **Concept reaction** | *Not a test of improvement.* It records only which fields people ask about or distrust. | — | Measured improvement needs a later task **with** an evidence card vs **without** (R09). |

Report as "k of 8 participants" with the sample's limits stated. **Do not** quote percentages or claim validation.
